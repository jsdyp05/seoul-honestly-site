Privacy Policy — Seoul, Honestly

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Seoul, Honestly ("the app") is made by Yun Minsu (윤민수), an individual developer in Korea.

1. What we collect

We don't collect personal information. There's no account, no login, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

2. Data stored on your device

Your checklist progress, saved places and trip settings are stored only on your device. We never see them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

3. Online features

Most of the app works offline. A few features request public data from third-party services when you're online:

  • Exchange rates: Frankfurter (frankfurter.app)
  • Weather and air quality: Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com)

These requests include no personal information. Like any web request, they reveal your device's IP address to the service. Their privacy policies apply.

4. Links to other apps

Tapping "Open in Naver Map" or "Open in Kakao Map" opens that app or website. Once you leave our app, that service's privacy policy applies.

5. Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect any data from anyone.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
